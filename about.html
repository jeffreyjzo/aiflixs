About AI Flixs

The streaming home for AI‑made films.

AI Flixs is where a new generation of filmmakers publishes work made with AI — and where audiences discover it first. Think Netflix meets Instagram: every creator gets a page, every film gets an audience.

🎬 For viewers

  • Free films — watch instantly, supported by ads.
  • Premieres — unlock pay-per-view films priced by their creators; one unlock gives you 7 days of unlimited watching.
  • Every film streams in crisp 1080p-to-4K from a global CDN.

🎥 For creators

  • Your own creator page with your films, followers, and view counts.
  • Set your own price — free with ads, or $2.99 to $100 per unlock.
  • Upload up to 20 GB per film (1080p minimum, 4K maximum). Your file streams straight to our video CDN — fast, resumable, reliable.
  • Every submission is human-reviewed before it goes live.

🚀 The future

AI filmmaking is the fastest-moving creative frontier on the planet. AI Flixs exists to give that movement a stage — and to make sure the people pushing it forward get paid for it.
